Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, updating your reader profile, purchasing coin packs, or sending messages. This includes your email address, username, reading history, and saved bookmarks.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is strictly used to synchronize your reading progress, enable community interactions, process payments, and improve your reading experience.

3. Data Storage & Security

User data is encrypted and securely managed via Firebase authentication and cloud databases. Offline preferences are cached locally on your device.

4. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to request a copy of your personal data or delete your account at any time via the Nive Settings page.

5. Contact Data Officer

For privacy inquiries, please contact privacy@niveapp.com.
